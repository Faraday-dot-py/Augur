## Pre-registered (before any main run)
Task A. PASS if, at N=2e4 on the held-out seeds, for tiled arms: (i) per-body error at tick 100 of bodies that are outside the box in truth is within 2x of the error of in-box bodies, (ii) the count of tracers at r>70 per 400 is within 2x of truth, gate <=2 per 400 (single grid 149; truth 0), (iii) fraction of bodies outside the box at t=300..1000 within 2x of truth (truth .19-.21), (iv) band 28-32 reversal fraction <=1% (truth ~0). KILL if tiled is no better than the single grid on (ii) and (iii).
Prediction A: in the tracer scene tiles recover the force (r>70 per 400 falls from ~149 to <10) because the cluster mass sits inside the domain; in the BH scene escapees are ballistic at ~c with p >> mc so zero force costs little position error (escapee error ratio ~1-2).
Task B hypotheses (outcome predicted for each): H1 edge-coupled core drift: T8/T16 keep the core within a few units of truth to t=1000 and frac outside box stays ~.2; single grids empty the box. H2 momfix beyond the grid: the residual force on the core is raw core bias minus the global mean, so when most bodies get zero grid force the correction is under-applied to the core; predicts S1nm drifts differently (sign/size) and the net force on the in-box set is nonzero (probe). H3 static cell-list cap in the CUDA-graph pair path truncates the dense-core pair force: S1g differs from S1 at N=2e4 (not at N=1e5, which uses `adapt`). H4 U-Net force error in the dense core (momentum non-conserving raw force, then spread by momfix): probe parts show the U-Net term carries the net core bias. H5 recurrent field / mass loss: mass on grid vs in-box count.

Smoke (job 3837, N=3000, 120 ticks) already seen before the main runs: raw (momfix off) single-grid force has a mean per-body bias of ~8-9 on the core (exact mean ~0.1), carried by the U-Net term (kernel and pair terms track exact); momfix removes a global mean of ~6; S1nm drifts 14 units in 100 ticks (S1 0.8). This motivates H2/H4 and is not a result at the scale of interest (N=2e4).
