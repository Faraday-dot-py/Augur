# Escapees outside the single grid and BH-core drift: tiled re-runs (2026-10-21)

Branch `scatter-escape-core-drift` (scatter-edge-fix 64a2b4d + tiling a6dc0f9 + far-kernel-fix 2d6d8f7, merged; two small conflicts resolved). Model = Exp E `ms_kp_pot_v_g128` with the learned ghost closure (`ms_kp_pot_v_g128_ghost`, Polaris ~/edge_ck/ghost_lsq.pt). Definitions: N = number of massive bodies; err@k = mean per-body position error vs exact fp64 truth at tick k (sim units, 1 tick = 0.1 time units); r = distance from origin; "box" = the single grid's [-32,32]^2; momfix = the model's removal of the mass-weighted mean acceleration; P = total relativistic momentum sum p; core = bodies within 10 units of the shrinking-sphere density centre.
Code: `scripts/escape_roll.py` (rollouts + probes), `scripts/escape_analyze.py`, job scripts `scripts/polaris_escape/`, raw results `results/escape/`.

## Arms
S0 single G=128 grid, ghost closure, shipped kernel; S1 same with R1b kernel; S1nm S1 with momfix off; S1g S1 on the default CUDA-graph pair path (static candidate cap fixed at tick 0, see Finding 0) instead of the exact `adapt` pair term; T4/T8/T16 tiled strict G=128 copies (32-wide tile, halo 16, ghosts, coarse far term cell 2, R1b kernel; domain extent 128/256/512); T8w 8x8 tiles of 64 (G=192, extent 512; not strict G=128, the user's "64 tiles, extent 512" cannot be both); T4nm/T8nm tiled with the global momfix off. ## Pre-registered (before any main run)
Task A. PASS if, at N=2e4 on the held-out seeds, for tiled arms: (i) per-body error at tick 100 of bodies that are outside the box in truth is within 2x of the error of in-box bodies, (ii) the count of tracers at r>70 per 400 is within 2x of truth, gate <=2 per 400 (single grid 149; truth 0), (iii) fraction of bodies outside the box at t=300..1000 within 2x of truth (truth .19-.21), (iv) band 28-32 reversal fraction <=1% (truth ~0). KILL if tiled is no better than the single grid on (ii) and (iii).
Prediction A: in the tracer scene tiles recover the force (r>70 per 400 falls from ~149 to <10) because the cluster mass sits inside the domain; in the BH scene escapees are ballistic at ~c with p >> mc so zero force costs little position error (escapee error ratio ~1-2).
Task B hypotheses (outcome predicted for each): H1 edge-coupled core drift: T8/T16 keep the core within a few units of truth to t=1000 and frac outside box stays ~.2; single grids empty the box. H2 momfix beyond the grid: the residual force on the core is raw core bias minus the global mean, so when most bodies get zero grid force the correction is under-applied to the core; predicts S1nm drifts differently (sign/size) and the net force on the in-box set is nonzero (probe). H3 static cell-list cap in the CUDA-graph pair path truncates the dense-core pair force: S1g differs from S1 at N=2e4 (not at N=1e5, which uses `adapt`). H4 U-Net force error in the dense core (momentum non-conserving raw force, then spread by momfix): probe parts show the U-Net term carries the net core bias. H5 recurrent field / mass loss: mass on grid vs in-box count.

Smoke (job 3837, N=3000, 120 ticks) already seen before the main runs: raw (momfix off) single-grid force has a mean per-body bias of ~8-9 on the core (exact mean ~0.1), carried by the U-Net term (kernel and pair terms track exact); momfix removes a global mean of ~6; S1nm drifts 14 units in 100 ticks (S1 0.8). This motivates H2/H4 and is not a result at the scale of interest (N=2e4).

## Results (Polaris jobs 3840 A, 3841 B, 3844 C, 3845 D, 3846 F, 3847 E, 3848 G; logs `results/escape/*.log`; about 1.1 GPU-hours on up to 2 GPUs)

### Task A: are escapees recoverable by tiling? Force yes, trajectories no.
Static force on massless tracers (job 3844, N=2e4 cluster, rings r 20..150; median inward force model/exact, momfix on; t = ticks the cluster was rolled first). Single grid: 0.03 beyond r=35 (zero force outside the box). Tiled (strict G=128, + far, R1b kernel; extent 128 / 256 / 512 for 4x4 / 8x8 / 16x16): ~0.99 out to the domain edge at t=0 (T8: .97 at r=100), but after the core has drifted (t=20-100) the ratio at r=60-100 falls to .3-.7 and goes negative for r>=100-150 (-2 to -4). With momfix OFF the ratio stays .98-1.01 at every r and tick. So the tiled force is right, and the global momfix (a uniform shift) spoils it far out. The old shipped kernel with tiles gives -3 to -45 beyond r=60 (the far-kernel bug); tiles without the far term (T8nf) give 0.0-0.1 beyond the box.
Tracer scene (100 ticks, 2000 tracers, seeds 4738/9000/12000), tracers at r>70 out of 2000: truth 0; single grid 781/749/741 (S0), 791/756/747 (S1); tiled T4 381/325/359, T8 384/322/382, T8w 379/315/372, T16 358/321/382. A 2.0-2.3x reduction, not the gate (<=2 per 400; tiled ~65-75 per 400, single ~150). Domain size 128..512 makes no difference. Band 28-32 reversal fraction: tiles .84-.86% vs single .82% vs truth .50% (seed 4738; seeds 9000/12000: 2.4%/1.2% vs .58/.50): tiles do not change the reversal rate (in-grid edge residue of the ghost closure).
BH collapse N=2e4, fraction of bodies outside the 64-box at t=100/300/500/1000 (truth .185/.194/.199/.214): S1 .205/.254/.822/.996, T8 .197/.228/.885/.999 (other seeds same). Fraction outside the tiled DOMAIN at t=1000: T8 .254, T8w .24, T16 .237 (truth .214): tiles keep the particles inside the domain, the single grid loses them. Mean position error per body at t=100 by truth class (seed 4738): in-box 9.8 (S1) / 9.3 (T8); outside box 56 / 56; escapees (r>70) 52 / 54: ratio escapee/in-box 5.3x single, 5.9x tiled (seed 9000 5.0x/5.0x, 12000 5.7x/6.2x); at t=300 6.9x / 8.4x. The 2x criterion fails for every arm; tiles improve in-box bodies (35.8 -> 29.3 at t=300) but not escapees (247 -> 245). N=1e5 (seed 4738): same pattern (T8 outside tiled domain .23 at t=1000 vs single 1.0).
Why escapees stay wrong although the tiled force is right: their error is made inside the box in the first 20-50 ticks (error already 3.5-3.7 outside the box at t=20 in all arms), while the cluster collapses and the in-grid force differs from truth; a body at ~c keeps that velocity error and its position error then grows linearly. Tiles cannot undo it.
Verdict A (pre-registered): static outside-box force PASS (.03 -> .9-.99); trajectory recovery FAIL (escapee error 5-8x in-box vs 2x gate; r>70 tracers 2.0-2.3x below single but not within 2x of truth 0); no domain-size dependence.

### Task B: core drift is not edge-coupled; it is a model momentum error present in every arm
Core centre (shrinking sphere; truth stays within 1 unit of the origin to t=1000, seed 4738): S1 (-0.7,1.6)@100, (-4.3,16.6)@300, (-6.4,27)@400, then the core dissolves (bodies within 10 units 14000 -> 1900 at t=500). T8/T16/T8w (core at a tile corner, domain edge 128-256 away): (-0.7,1.5)@100, (-3.6,13.4)@300, (-4.9,22.8)@400, (-7,34.5)@500, (-14,64)@700, (-41,117)@1000, and the core stays compact (r50 3.1-3.3, n10 14100) the whole time. Speed ~0.07 units/tick in +y, the same in S1 and tiled. Rotating the initial condition by 90/180 degrees or shifting it by 0.125, 0.25, 3.0, (3.25,-2.75) (job 3848, 6 variants): drift stays +y at the same speed (S1 (-5.5,15.7)@300 for rot90, T8 (-4.5,13.2)): the push is fixed in the grid frame, set by neither the scene orientation nor the sub-cell phase. Same in all 3 seeds and at N=1e5. H1 (edge-coupled) REFUTED.
Force breakdown (probes, model force on its own state vs exact fp64, N=2e4, seed 4738, t=100, bodies within 15 units of the centre): exact mean acceleration (0.07,-0.67); model raw (momfix off) (-0.5,+35.8), i.e. a bias of 36 in +y. Parts: kernel FFT term (-0.04,-0.44) = exact; pair term 0.0; U-Net term (-0.45,+36.3). The U-Net potential-gradient term carries all of it. After momfix the core bias is still 9.1 (13x the exact core acceleration). Net force in the box sum m*a: model 5.5e5 vs exact -3.6e3 (150x). H4 (U-Net term in the dense core) CONFIRMED.
momfix (H2): S1nm (momfix off) drifts 14-17 units by t=100 and has emptied the box by t=300 (total |P| 2e6 vs truth 3e3). momfix removes ~75% of the bias and leaves ~26% (9-10 of 36). Why: the mean is over all bodies, so bodies outside the box (zero grid force in the single grid) dilute it. Opt-in `in` arms (S1in/T8in subtract the in-box mean from in-box bodies only; S1un the in-box mean of the U-Net term only): at N=2e4 no effect on core drift (S1in (-6.1,27.2)@400 vs S1 (-6.4,27.1)); at N=1e5 large: S1in/S1un outside the box .33/.32 at t=1000 vs S1 1.0, core intact (n10 64600 of 74600 at t=1000), drift (-12.9,22.3)@1000; T8in (-26,72)@1000 vs T8 (-74,99). The N dependence is not understood. H2: PARTLY.
Total momentum is conserved only through momfix (tiled |P_all| = 3.2e3 = truth at all times) while the in-box momentum is 1.5-1.8e5 vs truth 1.1e4 at t=100: a large internal violation hidden by the global subtraction.
CUDA-graph pair-cap (H3, arm S1g): core drift, outside fraction, reversal, escapee errors and probes within noise of S1 at N=2e4 (core (-5.6,27.0)@400 vs (-6.4,27.1); bias 38 vs 37). FLAG: the static candidate cap fixed at tick 0 in the graph path (`_cap_now`) exists and was not seen to matter in this scene; N=1e5 uses `adapt` anyway.
Mass / recurrent field (H5): grid mass tracks the in-box count (smoke probe), no growth: not a source.

### Ranked fix candidates (research only)
1. Remove the net force of the U-Net term in the model, not by momfix: soft loss on the per-scene sum_i m_i a_i of the U-Net output (allowed: soft penalty), or a learned zero-sum head. Evidence: all of the bias is in the U-Net term; kernel and pair terms are exact; bias fixed in the grid frame.
2. Rotation averaging (force evaluated on the scene rotated by 0/90/180/270, rotated back, averaged): the bias is a fixed grid direction so it cancels at first order; 4x force cost, O(N). Cheap diagnostic of whether the bias is an anisotropy.
3. In-box momfix (opt-in `in`): core intact to t=1000 at N=1e5 but no effect at 2e4; not shippable until the N dependence is explained.
4. Dense-core input range (bias biggest in the collapsed core, far above training density): density normaliser/cap or training on collapsed states; untested.
Not worth doing: more tiling or larger domains (no effect on drift or escapee error), far-term variants, ghost closure changes.
