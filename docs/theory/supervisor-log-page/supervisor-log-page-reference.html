Terms

  • err@20: position error per body at tick 20, simulation units. dE: energy drift, |dE/N| per particle.
  • beta: force-bias statistic for a tier-0 model by density band (sparse, mid, dense). Exact definition is in the experiment log, not restated here.
  • tier 0: the fine-scale model. upper tiers: coarser moment and potential levels above it. hole N: numbered design risk in docs/theory/heterogeneous-tiered.md.
  • scatter-gravity-clustered-baseline (legacy: Exp E): the plain tier 0 checkpoint every comparison uses.

Settled

  • LJ energy drift comes from the velocity and recurrent inputs. Removing them holds drift at or under 0.013.
  • A gradient-of-energy mesh helps LJ modestly and adds nothing for 2D gravity (5.6x slower, no benefit).
  • The 9x solid LJ error was mostly integrator time step. Retraining at dt 0.0125 gives solid err@20 0.0029 against an exact-force floor of 0.0028 (1.8x cost). Cached pair substeps keep 0.0035 at 1.24x cost.
  • Teacher forcing suffices for tiers (gap 1.056 +/- 0.051).
  • Tile-route sparse activation is 4.7x faster at a 1024-unit span with 12.5% occupancy.
  • Tier 0 from scratch is the first to tile better than it runs untiled (0.54x). Matched-budget retrain (60,000 iterations) passes every pre-registered line: err@20 1.13x / 1.12x baseline, mid beta -0.118 / -0.052, tiled over untiled 0.95. The earlier mid-density bias was mostly undertraining.
  • Contact head gives no benefit; the existing contact option stays.
  • Hole 1 is closed.
  • Seam / owner-window hypothesis ruled out: particles that changed owner window carry 0.6-3% of the energy-work error and a stationary probe shows no force step at a seam.
  • README figure edge artifact was inference-side (kernel potential zero-padded at the grid border); fixed with a one-cell extension, weights unchanged (3756e2a, 66cbc29).

Killed

  • Every tier 1 and above arm on the far-field target. Best far_rel 0.547 against a 0.5 pass line.
  • Joint fine-tune of all tiers fails only on energy drift (0.048 to 0.064 against 0.010).
  • Every contact-head arm (six tried): tunnelling up to 100%, divergence, or no gain over the existing contact option.
  • Harvested-window tier 0 fine-tunes: accuracy collapse or a mid-density bias.
  • Tiers on the hole-9 comparison lost to the wide grid on accuracy and cost.
  • Density-stratified harvest at 18.75k iterations: err@20 1.34x baseline, mid beta worse.
  • End-to-end hierarchy (tier 0 with external field from upper tiers), both with the 10k-iteration and the matched-budget tier 0: joint fine-tune 1.8-2.4x the tiled baseline at tick 20, drift 3.5-13x. Gap is the field-aware tier 0 in 64-unit windows, not the upper-tier supply.
  • Planetesimal-disk proxy for our unit-mass models: err@100 1.73, energy drift 1.23 vs 0.003 reference, disk survivors 0.00 vs 0.39.
  • Retrain of the matched tier 0 without velocity and recurrent inputs (both seeds): tick-20 error 1.56-1.85x baseline, drift 6.0-7.3x (lines 1.2x and 2x).
  • Local work-bias and clump-centre loss terms: no gain over the control fine-tune on either seed (short fine-tunes, a weak test).
  • Frozen-base residual corrector: tick-20 error 1.60-1.92x baseline (line 1.25x), drift 4.7-5.3x; the head barely learned.
  • Tiled closed-loop tier 0 with halo exchange: fails drift (3.9-4.8x vs line 2.5x) and mid-density bias by 0.003, though position error passes.
  • Energy-similarity term in the tiled closed-loop fine-tune: stopped at the early gates on both seeds (seed 4738 drift halved but tick-20 error 1.29x; seed 4739 drift worse, error 1.62x).

Open

  • Mid-density bias in the earlier window-trained tier 0 checkpoints was real, not a test artefact (imposed-field re-score). Undertraining is now the supported cause (matched-budget run, one seed).
  • Matched-budget tier 0 caveats: sparse beta sits on its 0.15 limit, dense 16-32 beta +0.48 near the 0.5 kill line, plain dE@100 0.03 vs 0.014.
  • Energy drift stays about 4x baseline in the tiled closed-loop tier 0 even though own-state force error (0.039-0.041) is below the baseline (0.052). Audited at 01:15: the metric is valid, the baseline's 0.010 is flattered (exact pairwise force through the same step drifts 0.016-0.019), and the new model loses about 0.035 of energy systematically in close-pair potential energy. Energy-similarity term stopped at its gates on both seeds.
  • Visited-state effect: free-rollout force error was 1.5-1.6x baseline at the model's own states against +6-12% teacher-forced for the earlier tier 0; the halo exchange with closed-loop training closes the position gap but not the drift.
  • Per-body-mass training: design, curriculum and trainer committed, not submitted (needs a free GPU; GPU path untested).
  • Overnight showcase renders (jobs 4053-4056) completed; the showcase videos agent (running) is downloading, rendering and reviewing them, with a 1e10 render added. README figure still shows an unexplained interior force offset of about 1.55x against exact.
  • Joint fine-tune drift: the co-adaptation explanation is unconfirmed (one seed per arm). Frozen sequential training stays the default; joint fine-tune again gave nothing over sequential. A regularised joint variant is untested.
  • Not started: refinement tier, error head and calibration, object collisions on a trained model.
