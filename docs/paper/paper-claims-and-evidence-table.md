Requirements (docs/paper/core-requirements.md:3-6): generalization (any particle sim), tileable (one model, bigger area), O(N) cost, interpretability (rules recoverable; marked UNTESTED there).

Definitions: err@k = mean per-body position error at tick k against an exact simulation, simulation length units, lower is better. dE@k = relative energy drift at tick k. BL = the tiled scatter-field baseline a result is divided by. Ratio rows are candidate over control; below 1 is better. "Line" = the pre-registered pass/kill criterion. "Held-out seeds" 9100/9200/9300, "fresh seeds" 12000/12100/12200, training seed 4738 unless stated.

Markers (last column, Pilot decision 2026-11-17): validated = several seeds or sizes, a control and an approved pre-registered line; single-seed = one training seed or one run; worker-proposed-line = verdict rests on lines the user did not approve; post-hoc-amended = verdict depends on a line or rule changed after the result; speed-only = time and memory measured, no accuracy; unvalidated = no result, design only, or numbers not checked against a raw file. Markers were set from the table's strength column and are upgraded only after the Q4 review (last section).

Release policy (Pilot decision 2026-11-17): no raw result files are released. The paper cites result tables built from the saved files, not the files. File paths below are for the author's and reviewers' checking and are not paper citations; raw files kept outside git (~/polaris-mcp-files, Polaris) stay unreleased and are listed in paper-gap-list.md only as a local-record gap.

Strength scale: strong = several seeds or sizes, reviewer-confirmed, control present; one-seed = one training seed or one run; untested = designed or prepared, no result. A KILL or INCONCLUSIVE row is rated by how well the negative or open result is supported.
