How to read this

  • SIM (left panel): exact softened gravity, every pair summed, leapfrog. This is the ground truth, not a model.
  • MODEL (middle panel): bodies from the tiled network, in lockstep with the sim. Faint orange = where the sim has that body; the grey link is the error. The surface is the tiles' potential joined at the cores.
  • Ghost bodies: a neighbour's bodies inside a tile's 16-unit halo are copied in (rings, tinted by the receiving tile; gold arrows between tiles). Key g.
  • Halo potential: each tile's recurrent potential halo is overwritten by its neighbours' interiors (cyan). Key x.
  • Far term: the coarse global grid (cell 2) holds all mass; its learned-kernel potential minus the tile's own box is added to the tile's kernel input and output (orange arcs). Key f.
  • U-Net of the inspected tile, one set of weights shared by all tiles. Click a tile to inspect it. Slider: channel, -1 overlays all 32.

Exact operations (no learned parameters): CIC scatter and gather, FFT convolution, finite-difference gradient, avg-pool and bilinear resampling for the far term, ghost and halo bookkeeping, momentum re-centring, Verlet. Learned: the U-Net, the kernel K(r) (2-64-64-1 MLP, 4,417 parameters, retrained to the exact softened law out to r = 1500 and then frozen) and the short-range pair MLP. A tile slab stacks, front to back: potential, far term, local-kernel potential, density; keys 1-5 isolate one.

Toggling a mechanism restarts the model from the tick on screen with that mechanism changed (the exact sim is unaffected), so the error plot shows what it contributed. The model is not conservative: its error against the sim grows with time (chaotic N-body dynamics amplifies any difference).
