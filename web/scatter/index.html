Bounce scatter-field model

Bodies are scattered onto a 128×128 grid, a learned kernel and UNet turn the grid into a potential, and each body gathers its acceleration from it (plus a learned short-range pair term), integrated with velocity Verlet. Runs in plain JavaScript, ~0.4 s per tick. ← other models
