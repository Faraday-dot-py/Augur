Bounce

Three learned physics models, running live in the browser as plain JavaScript ports of their PyTorch checkpoints, plus a 3D walkthrough of the grid model.

Token model

General-purpose: one token per ball, a learned pair force, wall force and gravity drive a bouncing-ball arena. Click a ball to unfold its computation in 3D.

open →

Central-force model

Scales to 10,000+ bodies: a single learned pairwise gravity law, integrated with velocity Verlet, compared live against ground-truth gravity.

open →

Scatter-field model

A grid architecture: bodies are scattered onto a field, a learned kernel and UNet produce a potential, and bodies gather their acceleration from it. Watch the learned potential evolve under the bodies.

open →

Scatter-field walkthrough

A 3D, step-by-step tour of one forward pass of the scatter-field model: scatter, kernel, UNet, gather and integrate, as spatial tensors with linked commentary.
